    What this game shows

    You are rostering a warehouse. The labour you need depends on the average time an order takes to pick, and you cannot time every order, so you pay to time a few and work from their average. That average, x-bar, is not the truth: it is one draw from a whole pile of averages you might have got. The pile sits on the true mean, and its spread is sigma divided by the square root of n, so each extra order you time buys less precision than the one before. Because timing costs money and being wrong costs money, there is a point where one more order is not worth it. Finding that point is the game.

    Nothing you do here is stored or sent anywhere.
